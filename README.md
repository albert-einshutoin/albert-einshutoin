## 🧬 Core

AI is lowering the barrier between thought and product.

More ideas can now become working software.  
More people can turn intent into systems.

That makes shared infrastructure, clear context, and reproducible engineering more important than ever.

That is where I build.

---

## 🛰️ Philosophy

I want to open-source not only code, but the thinking behind reliable systems.

- Humans hold intent, constraints, and context  
- AI can accelerate implementation  
- OSS makes the result reusable, inspectable, and accessible  

> AI makes ideas easier to build.  
> OSS makes good ideas easier to share.

---

## ⚙️ What I Do

I turn engineering ideas into tools, frameworks, emulators, and optimization engines.

- Split context so humans and AI can collaborate safely  
- Build OSS infrastructure for AI-native development  
- Create reproducible systems for security, CI, media, i18n, and edge operations
- Optimize flows, economics, and reliability, not just code  

---

## 🧠 Why Low Layer?

The lower the layer, the greater the impact.

One abstraction can support:
- thousands of developers  
- millions of users  
- billions of requests  

> I don’t build apps.  
> I build what apps depend on.
